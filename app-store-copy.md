# Nexus — app store listing copy

## Google Play (English, en-GB)

**Title (30)**  
Nexus — team work, in sync

**Short description (80)**  
Plan tasks, run boards and see delivery truth. Team work that fits in your pocket.

**Full description (4000)**  
Nexus keeps the plan and the people in one place. Boards, lists and timelines all read the
same dataset, so a card moved on your phone is already moved on the desktop of everyone
else — no refresh, no “which version is right?” conversation.

BUILT FOR THE WAY WORK ACTUALLY MOVES
• Four columns, drag to move a task from idea to done
• Every card carries its owner, due date and status colour
• Subtasks, attachments and time tracking live on the task itself
• Offline-safe edits: change anything on the train, it syncs when you land

SEE THE TRUTH ABOUT DELIVERY
• Live throughput, cycle time and workload — updated as tasks complete
• Sprint progress on the home screen, no report request needed
• Escalation rules ping the owner before a deadline is missed, not after

DESIGNED TO BE READ
• Light and dark themes, both contrast-checked
• Large tap targets and a bottom tab bar for one-handed use
• Motion respects the reduced-motion setting of your phone

TEAM, WITH THE RIGHT ACCESS
• Owner, admin, manager, member and guest roles
• Invite by email; guests only see the project you share with them
• Audit trail of every invite and role change

PRIVACY FIRST
• Two-factor authentication and SSO on paid plans
• Data stored in the EU or UK region you choose
• Export or delete your workspace whenever you like

Nexus is free for small teams. Paid plans unlock unlimited automation runs, workload
reporting and SSO. Cancel any time — monthly seats are never refunded mid-period, annual
seats are refunded pro rata in the first 30 days.

Questions? support@nexus.app
